BREAK SURGEON

BREAK SURGEON is the rhythm half of JUNGLE OS, a drum & bass live rig of two M-VAVE FM-1s. It plays sliced breaks and six synthesized drum models (PUNCH KICK, CRACK SNARE, GHOST SNARE, METAL HATS, BREAK PERC, IMPACT FX) on one sample-accurate 7-track sequencer with velocity, probability, ratchets, microtiming, swing and parameter locks. On top of that: fills that return cleanly, ghost-note generation, mutation with anchors and undo, BREAK-LOCK, eight held performance FX (ROLL, CHOP, REVERSE, GLITCH, DESTROY, FREEZE, TAPE STOP) with RETURN, sweetspot graphs for the rhythm and the kit, six scenes and the DRUM PRESSURE master bus. It works alone, and with ABYSS ENGINE on a second FM-1 over JUNGLE LINK.

New in 0.7: a rebuilt kit (a kick with a held 56 Hz body, a snare with a clap layer, brighter hats), a console EQ on the bus, the break ducking under the synth kick, six synthesized classic breaks (AMEN, THINK, APACHE, FUNKY DRUMMER, HOT PANTS, ROLLER: the patterns only, not the recordings; ALGO on the BREAK track's SOUND page) and 18 grooves, among them LIQUID, NEURO and two jungle edits.

New in 0.6: ROUTES (hold SEQ: the playhead's path through the break, or one you draw), DNA (breed grooves in a family tree), ENERGY (hold HOME: every step a level 1–4, one knob for build-ups, shared with ABYSS ENGINE) and TAPE DECAY.

New in 0.5: a step sequencer first. The white keys are always the selected track's steps, the black keys the tracks; the live tools sit on held buttons (hold FX: performance FX incl. TAPE STOP; hold ENV: four fill types; hold LFO: variations A-D and a FORM such as A B A C; hold ARP: scenes, patterns and mutes). REC records live, hold REC erases, trig conditions (1:4, FILL, PHRASE END …) on every step, DENSITY and HUMANIZE, 8-step undo and redo, autosave after STOP.

New in 0.4: GROOVES. ALGO on the SEQUENCE page loads one of 14 classic drum & bass grooves (2-STEP, STEPPA, HALF-TIME, DANCEHALL, FOOTWORK, ROLLER …) and re-chops the break to it.

New in 0.3.1: the built-in break plays the Amen pattern at 170 BPM, synthesized from BREAK SURGEON's own drums (the pattern only, not the copyrighted 1969 recording).

New in 0.2: a drum & bass kit. The kick is short and punchy at 55 Hz with a beater click. The snare has a body, a crack, a snap and a tail, and the ghosts are that snare played softly, a little late. Every drum's tone follows how hard it is hit. DRUM PRESSURE adds a room, a sampler's 12-bit grit and a glue that makes the kit denser instead of quieter. The controls are the same as ABYSS ENGINE's, and the screen shows what every key and encoder does, also with SEL or SAVE held.

Version 0.7.2. Not yet tested on a physical FM-1. It is tested in software: 620 engine checks, audio-regression renders, fm1-base's host simulator and fm1-emu, an instruction-level emulator that runs the real firmware file. Installing it is at your own risk; the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back. Back up first.

  • Try it in the browser: the same code the FM-1 runs, with sound and Web MIDI. No FM-1 needed.
  • Manual: every page, button and key.
  • Install: from Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB.
  • Download breaksurgeon-0.7.2.fwsc: for the command-line installer, python3 base/tools/fm1_install.py breaksurgeon-0.7.2.fwsc.
  • Earlier versions: 0.7.1, 0.7.0, 0.6.0, 0.5.0, 0.4.0 (the 0.4 controls), 0.3.1 (before the grooves), 0.3.0 (the synthetic two-step break), 0.2.0 (the 0.2 screens), 0.1.0-dev (the first kit and controls).
  • ABYSS ENGINE, the bass half, and JUNGLE OS, both together.
  • Source code on GitHub.

The FM-1 has no audio input: breaks are prepared on a computer (tools/sample-prep/jo_sample_prep.py) and sent over USB as a sample bank. Without a bank it plays its built-in test break. Use only audio you have the rights to.
